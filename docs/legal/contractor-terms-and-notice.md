### 1. How pros join

- **Pros sign up themselves.** We don't build contractor lists by scraping websites or copying public databases, and we don't email contractors who haven't signed up.
- To sign up you give us: business name, contact name, business email and phone, service area (ZIP codes), trade or specialties, and your **contractor license number** (and the state that issued it).
- You must be authorized to bind the business, and the information you give must be true and kept up to date.
- We may approve, decline, or remove any pro account. [NOTE: Decide on an approval step, for example a manual review before a pro can receive leads.]

### 2. Licenses and verification

- **You must hold every license your work requires.** In California, work worth $1,000 or more (labor and materials combined) generally requires a license from the Contractors State License Board (CSLB) (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 7048). Only accept leads you are licensed and qualified to do.
- **How we check.** We will check the license number you give us against the issuing board's official license lookup, including that the license is active and that the business name and classification match. [NOTE: How the lookup is done (manual or automated, and how often it's re-checked) is not decided yet.]
- **"Verified" badge.** A pro is shown as "Verified" only after a real license check has passed, and the badge means only that the license was active and matched when we last checked. It does **not** mean we vouch for your work, insurance, bonding, or reviews. Badges stay off until a real license lookup exists.
- You must tell us within 7 days if your license is suspended, expires, or changes. [CONFIRM] We may hide or remove your account if a check fails.

### 4. How homeowner leads are shared with you

- A lead is sent only when a homeowner asks to find a pro for their project.
- **The homeowner chooses what you see.** Before anything is sent, the homeowner sees exactly what pros will receive and can turn each item on or off. A lead may include the project's AI design, a design brief, the type of room, a budget range, timeline, and ZIP code.
- **The homeowner's contact details stay hidden until the homeowner picks you.** Until then, you communicate only through VisionBuild.
- The homeowner's **original room photo is shared only if the homeowner turns it on**. [CONFIRM]
- Use lead information **only** to respond to and quote that homeowner's project. Don't sell it, add the homeowner to marketing lists, or contact the homeowner outside VisionBuild unless they've chosen you and shared their contact details.
- AI designs and briefs are inspiration only. They are not plans, measurements, or a scope of work you can rely on. Confirm everything with the homeowner and, where needed, by a site visit.
- We don't guarantee any number or quality of leads, or that any homeowner will hire you.

### 5. Messaging and quotes

- You can message homeowners and send quotes through the VisionBuild inbox.
- Quotes are **yours**, not VisionBuild's. You are responsible for their accuracy and for any contract you sign with a homeowner, including everything California and local law requires for home improvement contracts (for example, required contract terms, notices, and down payment limits).
- Be honest, professional, and respectful. No spam, harassment, bait-and-switch pricing, or pressure tactics.
- Homeowners can Report and Block pros. We review reports and may remove leads, messages, or accounts.
- We may keep and review messages to deliver them, keep users safe, and handle reports.

### 6. VisionBuild's role

VisionBuild is a technology platform. **We are not a contractor**, we don't perform or supervise work, and we're not a party to any agreement between you and a homeowner.
