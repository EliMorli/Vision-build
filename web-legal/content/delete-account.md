# Delete your VisionBuild account

You can delete your VisionBuild account and the data that goes with it at any time. Deletion can't be undone.

## Option 1: In the app (fastest)

1. Open VisionBuild and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, read what will be deleted, and tap **Delete my account** to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to disconnect VisionBuild from your Apple ID.

## Option 2: On the web (if you can't use the app)

Go to **https://{{WEBSITE_DOMAIN}}/delete-account** and enter the email address on your account. We'll email you a single-use confirmation link that works for 24 hours. Nothing is deleted until you open the link and press the delete button on the confirmation page.

## Option 3: By email

Write to **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** from the email address on your account with the subject line "Delete my account". We'll confirm the request comes from you before deleting anything, and reply within 45 days.

## What we delete

- Your account, public profile and profile photo
- Your projects, room photos, room analysis and designs, from our database and from both private and public storage
- Public copies of your designs (they come off Explore)
- Your design briefs (Vi chats are never saved on our servers, so there is nothing to delete)
- Your settings, privacy choices and AI consent records
- Your XP, levels and badges
- Your pros waitlist signup
- Reports you filed and users you blocked
