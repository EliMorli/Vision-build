# VisionBuild support

Need help with VisionBuild? Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days.

## Common questions

- **How do I start a redesign?** Open the app, tap **+**, take or choose a photo of your room, then pick a style.
- **Who can see my photos?** Only you. Your original room photos are stored privately. If you make a project Public, only the AI-generated designs are shown on Explore.
- **How do I report a design or block someone?** On Explore, tap **⋯** on the design, then **Report this design** or **Block this user**.
- **How do I delete my account?** See [Delete your account]({{DELETE_URL}}).
